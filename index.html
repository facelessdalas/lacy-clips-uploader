Lacy Clips Uploader

A personal tool for one YouTube channel.

Lacy Clips Uploader is a small program that runs on the owner's own computer. It takes short vertical video clips that the owner has already made and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel, scheduled to go public at set times. It also reads the channel's own public and analytics numbers (views, watch time, audience retention) so the owner can see which clips work.

What the app does

  • Uploads video files to the signed-in owner's YouTube channel and sets their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the owner's own channel's video list and statistics, and YouTube Analytics reports for that channel.
  • Nothing else. It does not read other people's accounts, post comments, change channel settings or delete videos.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the channel signs in. There is no public sign-up, no other users and no server: the app runs locally and talks directly to Google's YouTube APIs.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. In short: the app keeps its Google sign-in token on the owner's computer, shares no data with anyone, and the owner can revoke access at any time.
