Privacy Policy

Lacy Clips Uploader · Last updated: 2026-10-17

Lacy Clips Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner, to upload video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses, what it does with it, and what it does not do.

What Google data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for these permissions (scopes):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos to the owner's channel and set their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly – to read the owner's own channel and video list (for example, to check that an upload finished and was scheduled).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly – to read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources), so the owner can see which clips perform best.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the features above: uploading and scheduling the owner's videos and showing the owner's own channel statistics. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

The app has no server and no database in the cloud. Its sign-in token (the credential Google gives the app after the owner agrees) is saved in a file on the owner's own computer. Channel statistics the app downloads are saved in local files on the same computer. Nothing is uploaded to any service other than YouTube/Google.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. No third party receives it. Nobody other than the owner has access to it.

TikTok data (Lacy clips, TikTok Content Posting API)

The app can also send the owner's own edited clips to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts. When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app asks for user.info.basic (to show which account is connected) and video.upload (to send a video file to the owner's inbox as a draft). The app never posts publicly by itself: the owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app. The TikTok sign-in token is saved in a file on the owner's own computer; nothing from TikTok is stored on any server, sold, shared or used for advertising. The owner can revoke access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services) and delete the local token file at any time.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the local token file and downloaded statistics from their computer at any time. After access is revoked, the app can no longer reach the owner's YouTube account.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone but its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
